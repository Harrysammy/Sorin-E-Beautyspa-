NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How do I make an appointment?+

Choose a treatment and submit the appointment form. You’ll be taken to WhatsApp with your details ready to send, so our team can confirm availability.

Are the prices final?+

Prices are starting/model prices. Treatment needs can vary, so please confirm your preferred service with us on WhatsApp before visiting.

Are you open on Sundays?+

No. Sorin E BeautySpa is open 24 hours from Monday through Saturday and closed on Sunday.

Can I book for two people?+

Yes. Select the number of guests on the booking form and let us know the treatments you’re both interested in.

Privacy policy

We only use the details you provide in the booking form to help you request an appointment through WhatsApp. We do not process payments or store card information on this website.
